# 📊 FMCG Sales Dashboard

## 🧾 Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing FMCG sales, profit, customer behavior, and regional performance.

## 🎯 Objective
To convert raw sales data into actionable insights for better business decisions.

## 📖 Key Insights
- 📊 $14.45M revenue with ~20% profit margin  
- 🛒 Wholesale channel drives highest sales  
- 🧍 B2B customers contribute ~81% profit  
- 🧴 Household category is top-performing  
- 🌍 Europe & USA lead in revenue  
- 📈 Sales peak in December (seasonal trend)  

## 📖 Storytelling
- 📊 Strong revenue and profit margin indicate a stable and profitable business  
- 🛒 Heavy reliance on wholesale shows channel-driven growth  
- 🧍 B2B dominance highlights importance of business clients  
- 🧴 Essential products drive consistent demand and revenue  
- 🌍 Global regions like Europe & USA contribute major share  
- 📈 Seasonal trends impact sales performance significantly  
- 🎯 High organic sales suggest strong product-market fit  
- 👨‍💼 Sales performance differences affect overall revenue  

## 🛠 Tools Used
Power BI | Excel | DAX  

## 📌 Conclusion
This dashboard acts as a decision-making tool by uncovering key sales trends, customer behavior, and business opportunities.
